Fire tomb is explicit in `/26又3`; fire extinction has separately scoped compatible corroboration in `易林補遺/1`. No categorical 易林 efficacy or trigram-direction rules are imported. 土巳/金丑 exceptions and distinct hidden/flying object scopes remain conditional. A structural 墓/绝 does not establish selected object, comprehensive strength, actor effectiveness, event outcome, medical conclusion or forecast date.

The electronic texts are not print-collated. No historical outcome, provider response, prediction or medical efficacy is validated. The matrix is bounded, not an exhaustive combination of every supported calendar date, subject and line pattern. Capacity maxima are observed sampled maxima, not a formal proof for every accepted input.
